How it plays

You build the base. The army runs itself. There are two resources: energy, which generators produce on their own, and metal, which you collect from map nodes and wrecks. Metal on the map runs out, so wrecks matter.

  1. Place a building

    Press an icon, drag it onto the map and let go. It starts working the moment it lands.

  2. Units build themselves

    Each building turns out units as fast as it can. You never queue or select.

  3. They march and fight

    Units head for the nearest enemy and attack what they meet. With no enemy units left, they go for buildings.

  4. Wrecks drop

    A destroyed unit leaves a wreck worth half its cost. Drones blow up on impact and leave nothing.

  5. Gatherers haul it home

    Forklift gatherers carry wrecks to the dropoff. Each team can field four at a time.

The units

Fixed camera, flat ground, no zoom. You read the fight by who is where.

Units in the current test build
DogA robot dog with a turret on its back. Walks on four legs.
TankTurret and barrel with recoil. Fires at ground targets.
HelicopterA twin-rotor gunship that flies above the field and fires homing rockets.
SAMA wheeled launcher on a truck. Tracks aircraft and shoots them down with homing missiles.
GathererA forklift that carries wrecks to the dropoff. Steers from the rear wheels, like the real thing.
FPV DroneFlies into its target and explodes. Leaves no wreck.

Iron River

A story campaign built for first-timers. Each episode teaches one unit or building, starting with the Gatherer, and every episode plays on its own map. Episode 1 is playable in the current test build.
