The game

The game has no sign-in with an email or password. Players pick a username, which other players see in online matches, along with the player's level and battle rating. Online matches also use your device's network connection to the game's servers.

The game will show ads and offer in-game purchases.

  • Ads: ads are provided by third-party ad networks. They may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details, only confirmation that a purchase was made.

Before the game is released on Google Play, this section will name the ad networks and any analytics tools and list exactly what each collects, and the same details will go into the Google Play data safety form. If we add anything else, we will list it here before it ships.
